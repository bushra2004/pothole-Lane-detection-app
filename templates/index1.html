AI CAR WITH REAL-TIME DETECTION OF DAMAGED ROADS & LANE DETECTION

⬆️

Abstract

Road safety and infrastructure maintenance are crucial for modern transportation. Accurate lane detection enhances autonomous driving, while identifying road damage enables timely repairs. Traditional inspections are inefficient, and existing methods struggle in adverse conditions. This project develops an automated system that processes uploaded road videos to detect lanes and road damage efficiently, improving safety and maintenance efforts.
